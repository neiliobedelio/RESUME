Brand Development · 2024

Brand Strategy & Visual Identity — Orange County, NY Tourism

Synthesized qualitative and quantitative research — stakeholder interviews, community input, and visitor studies — into a brand strategy that differentiated this destination from neighboring markets, including New York City. Developed a visual identity system (color palette, typography, brand voice) and a messaging framework built around authentic local language — "fresh picked," "fresh state of mind" — to sharpen competitive positioning for the region's apple-orchard heritage.
