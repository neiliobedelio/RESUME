Brand Development · 2020

Brand Strategy & Stylescape Development — Visit Grand Prairie

Conducted on-site community immersion and stakeholder research to ground a bespoke destination brand strategy in authentic local character. Developed a stylescape — a visual alignment tool that communicates brand direction (color, typography, imagery, tone) before full identity development begins — to build stakeholder consensus early in the process. Produced a brand introduction video and teaser video to bring the strategy to life for client presentations.
